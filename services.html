Our Services

Everything your smile needs, all in one place

From preventive care to life-changing smile makeovers, we offer a full range of dental services — explained clearly, delivered gently.

General Checkups & Cleanings

Your routine checkup is the foundation of long-term oral health. We recommend visiting every six months so we can catch potential issues early — before they become painful or costly.

During your visit, we’ll perform a thorough exam, take digital X-rays when needed, and gently clean and polish your teeth. We’ll also screen for gum disease, oral cancer, and other concerns.

  • Comprehensive oral exam
  • Professional cleaning & polishing
  • Digital X-rays (low radiation)
  • Gum health assessment
  • Oral cancer screening

Cosmetic Dentistry

You deserve to love your smile. Our cosmetic dentistry services are designed to enhance the natural beauty of your teeth using subtle, natural-looking techniques.

We offer professional teeth whitening (in-office and take-home), porcelain veneers for a smile transformation, and dental bonding to repair chips, gaps, or discolouration in a single visit.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Dental bonding
  • Smile makeover consultations

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth without metal braces. Invisalign uses a series of clear, comfortable, removable aligners that gradually shift your teeth into place — and most people won’t even notice you’re wearing them.

Dr. Chen is a certified Invisalign provider. We use digital scanning to map out your treatment plan from start to finish, so you can see your future smile before you begin.

  • Virtually invisible aligners
  • Removable for eating & brushing
  • Digital treatment preview
  • Suitable for teens and adults

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies are stressful — getting help shouldn’t be. We reserve same-day appointments for urgent cases so you don’t have to wait in pain.

Whether it’s a severe toothache, cracked tooth, lost filling, or dental injury, call us right away. We’ll get you in quickly and provide immediate relief.
