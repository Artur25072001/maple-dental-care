About Our Practice

Meet the people behind your smile

We’re a team of dedicated professionals who truly love what we do — and it shows in every patient interaction.

Lead Dentist

Dr. Sarah Chen, DDS

Dr. Sarah Chen founded Maple Dental Care with a simple belief: that exceptional dentistry should also be genuinely kind. After earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Toronto, she spent over a decade honing her craft in both private practice and community clinics.

She’s passionate about staying at the forefront of modern dentistry — regularly attending continuing education in cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign treatment, and gentle care techniques for anxious patients.

When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Chen can be found exploring Toronto’s food scene with her family, running along the waterfront, or attempting to keep her houseplants alive.

Our Mission

Care that goes beyond the chair

We believe a dental visit should leave you feeling confident — not anxious. That’s why we’ve built our practice around three core principles: compassion in every interaction, clarity in every conversation, and excellence in every treatment.

We take time to listen, to explain your options in plain language, and to involve you in every decision about your care.
