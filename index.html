About Our Practice

Dentistry that feels like family

At Maple Dental Care, we believe a trip to the dentist should never be something to dread. Our team combines advanced technology with a warm, patient-first approach — so you and your family feel genuinely cared for at every visit.

Whether it’s a routine cleaning, a cosmetic consultation, or an unexpected toothache, Dr. Sarah Chen and our entire team are here to listen, explain, and treat you with the kindness you deserve.

What We Offer

Complete care for every smile

From preventive checkups to transformative cosmetic treatments, we bring the best of modern dentistry under one roof.

General Checkups & Cleanings

Thorough exams and gentle cleanings to keep your teeth healthy and your smile bright — recommended every six months for the whole family.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding — natural-looking treatments that enhance your confidence and help you love your smile again.

Invisalign

Clear aligners that straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably — a virtually invisible solution for teens and adults alike.

Emergency Dental Care

When pain strikes, we’re here. Same-day emergency appointments for toothaches, broken teeth, and other urgent needs.
